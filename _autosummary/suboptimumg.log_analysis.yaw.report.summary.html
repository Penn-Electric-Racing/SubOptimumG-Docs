suboptimumg.log_analysis.yaw.report.summary.axle_saturation(Ca0, alpha, p)[source]#

Saturation angles of one axle’s stiffness decay law.

Stiffness drops by fraction d where alpha slip^p = d / (1 - d); lateral force Ca0 slip / (1 + alpha slip^p) peaks at slip^p = 1 / (alpha (p - 1)) when p > 1.

Parameters:

  • Ca0 (float) – Linear-region stiffness (N/rad).

  • alpha (float) – Decay rate (rad^-p).

  • p (float) – Decay exponent.

Returns:

Drop angles and force peak.

Return type:

AxleSaturation
